# soft hearts v0.2.1

<p align="right"><a href="#zh"><img src="../images/lang-zh.svg" alt="中文" height="30"></a></p>

> 💗 Hello! v0.2.1 is a small fix on top of v0.2.0. It changes one thing, and it is something you may have missed without knowing it.

## What it fixes 🩹

On a new install of v0.2.0, her replies could never carry a small action: no heart, wave, nod, head tilt, clap, wink or pout. On the Character page, the switches under "What this model can do" stayed grey. The same happened to any model loaded for the first time since late November, for example when you first switched to Mumi or loaded a model of your own.

The cause: after a model loads, the app works out what that model can do (its "check-up") and keeps it, and everything that offers her small actions reads that stored check-up. In v0.2.0 the check-up was worked out but never kept. Now it is kept once, right after the model loads, and she can use the small actions again. The Character page no longer waits on "Reading this model's check…" for good either: it shows the switches, or says in a plain sentence why it cannot yet.

In a packaged copy (one built with the packaged option), the preinstalled Mumi and One had the same problem for a second reason: files inside the app's own package report a new time every time they are looked at, so the app could never tell which model they were, and nothing it keeps per model was kept for them. That is fixed too.

Who was affected: everyone who installed v0.2.0 fresh. If your models were already loaded with an older version, their check-up had been kept back then, and you did not see this.

## How to get it 📦

- New installs: install as usual with the one sentence in [START-HERE.md](../../START-HERE.md); you get this version.
- An existing install: ask your own Claude Code to "update soft hearts" (the update section of [START-HERE.md](../../START-HERE.md): `git pull` for a cloned folder, a new ZIP otherwise), then let it start her again. The check-up is made by itself the next time the model loads; there is nothing to click.
- Your chats, diary, what she knows about you and your settings are not touched.

## Nothing else changed

Everything else is exactly as in v0.2.0. What v0.2.0 added, and its known limits, are in [v0.2.0.md](v0.2.0.md).

## Honest limits ⚠️

- VRM models (One) still have no check-up, so she picks no small action in chat, and that part of the Character page says VRM support is still in progress. This was already so in v0.2.0.
- What was tried, each on this computer with a fresh profile: from source with Gene; in a packaged copy with Gene, Mumi and One (Gene and Mumi each made a heart; One loaded and was recognised as her own model; after a restart, what had been kept was found again). Not tried: a packaged build on another computer, and models supplied by users in the running app.

# soft hearts v0.2.1

> 💗 你好！v0.2.1 是在 v0.2.0 上的一个小修复，只改了一件事，你可能一直没注意到它坏了。

## 修了什么 🩹

新装的 v0.2.0 里，她的回复从来带不上小动作：比心、挥手、点头、歪头、鼓掌、眨一只眼、撅嘴，一个都没有。「角色」页「这个模型能做什么」下面的开关也一直是灰的。11 月下旬以来第一次载入的模型也一样，比如第一次换成 Mumi，或者第一次换上你自己的模型。

原因：模型载入后，软件会算出这个模型能做什么（它的"体检"）并存下来，给她挑小动作的地方读的都是存下来的这份体检。v0.2.0 算出来了，却没有存。现在模型一载入就存一次，她又能做这些小动作了。「角色」页也不会一直停在"正在读取这个模型的体检……"：要么显示开关，要么用一句白话说明为什么还不行。

在打包版里（用打包选项做出来的那一份），预装的 Mumi 和 One 还因为另一个原因有同样的问题：软件包里的文件每次被查看时都报一个新的时间，软件一直认不出它们是哪个模型，按模型保存的东西也就都没给它们存。这个也一起修好了。

谁受影响：所有新装 v0.2.0 的人。如果你的模型是用更早的版本载入过的，那时体检已经存好了，你不会遇到这个问题。

## 怎么装 📦

- 新装：照 [START-HERE.md](../../START-HERE.md) 用那一句话照常安装，装上的就是这一版。
- 已经装过的：让你自己的 Claude Code"更新 soft hearts"（[START-HERE.md](../../START-HERE.md) 里的更新一节：用 git 克隆的文件夹 `git pull`，否则用新的 ZIP），再让它把她重新打开。下次载入模型时会自己做体检，不用点任何东西。
- 你的聊天、日记、她对你的了解和设置都不会动。

## 别的都没变

其他一切和 v0.2.0 完全一样。v0.2.0 加了什么、有哪些已知的不足，见 [v0.2.0.md](v0.2.0.md)。

## 照实说的不足 ⚠️

- VRM 模型（One）还是没有体检，所以她聊天时一个小动作都不会挑，「角色」页这一块写的是 VRM 还在做。这在 v0.2.0 里就是这样。
- 试过的（都在这台电脑上、用全新的配置）：从源码运行，用 Gene；打包版里用 Gene、Mumi 和 One（Gene 和 Mumi 各比了一次心；One 能载入，也认得出是她自己的模型；重开软件后，存下的东西都还在）。没试过的：在别的电脑上用打包好的版本，以及用户自己的模型在运行中的软件里。
